# Reports

The questions a shop owner asks, answered from the book rather than from a
spreadsheet somebody keeps by hand.

## What is here

- **Profit and loss** — revenue, expenses and what is left, for a period you
  choose.
- **Aged receivables** — who owes you, and for how long.
- **Break-even** — what you must bill to cover what the shop costs to keep open.
- **Cash flow** — what has actually come in and gone out, which is not the same
  as what has been earned.
- **Client lifetime value** — what a customer has been worth, counting the work
  that reached them.
- **Quote funnel** — how many quotes became jobs.
- **Machine profitability** — what each printer earns, and what it earns per
  hour it is running.
- **Customer mix** — new against returning.
- **Throughput and quality** — how much finishes, how much is right first time,
  and what the reprints cost.
- **Material cost** — what you are paying per unit, and whether it has moved.
- **Capacity** — whether you can take the next job.

## Custom reports

Pick the columns and the period and Khayt builds the table. It exports, so a
figure can leave the app for an accountant without being retyped.

## Two figures that disagree on purpose

Pass rate and first-pass yield are both shown, because the gap between them is
the point: a shop that reprints until it passes has a pass rate near 100% and a
quality problem. First-pass yield collapses a reprint chain to one job, so the
difference is exactly the work you did twice and billed once.
