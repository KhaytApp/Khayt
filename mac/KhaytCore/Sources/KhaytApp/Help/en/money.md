# Money

What a job cost, what you charged, what has been paid, and what is owed.

## Owed, always on screen

The figure in the title bar is what the shop is owed, on every screen. It is not
a card halfway down a dashboard because it is the only figure that is true of
the whole book at once.

## Invoices

Raise one from a job. The document carries your shop's details, the customer's,
the work, the tax — and it is built by the same rules as the other app's, from
the same stylesheet, so the two cannot drift into looking like different
companies.

**Save PDF** writes it where you choose.

## Tax

Khayt computes VAT from your settings, and revenue is held **net of tax**
throughout. A few figures stay gross on purpose because that is what they mean —
what a customer actually hands over is one of them.

For Saudi shops, ZATCA's requirements are supported: the bilingual invoice and
the QR code that carries the invoice's own details.

## Payments

Record a payment against a job, in part or in full. A deposit is a deposit — it
is not counted as the whole job having been paid, and the cash-flow figures know
the difference.

## Expenses and waste

- **Expenses** — what the shop spends that is not a job: filament bought,
  electricity, maintenance, tools, shipping.
- **Waste** — a print that failed, what it was made of, what went wrong, and
  what it cost. Optionally taking the material off the spool, because a failed
  print consumed it just as surely as a good one.

Both screens are searchable by note, category, material or the job a cost was
booked to.
