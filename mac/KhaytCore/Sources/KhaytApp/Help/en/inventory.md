# The shelf

Every spool you have, what is left on it, and what it is costing you.

## What a card says

The material and colour, how much is left, and — where Khayt has enough history
to say — **when it will run out** at the rate you are using it. A spool that has
been open a while may also be flagged as needing drying.

A spool low enough to matter is marked, so the reorder decision is made by
looking rather than by counting.

## What it costs

Above the shelf is what the whole rack is worth and whether that has moved since
you bought it. It is on this screen rather than in the reports because this is
where you stand when you are deciding what to reorder.

It is hidden while you are searching: a total for the whole shelf sitting above
three filtered cards would be describing a different set from the one on screen.

## Filament comes off the spool

When a job records what it actually used, that weight comes off the spool it
used. That is what keeps the shelf honest without anybody counting — and it is
why recording actuals is worth the few seconds.

## Labels

**Book → Print labels** prints the whole rack. One spool's label is on its own
right-click menu. The labels carry what the spool is, so a rack stays legible
after the manufacturer's sticker has gone.

## Searching

The search box narrows the shelf by **material or colour**.
