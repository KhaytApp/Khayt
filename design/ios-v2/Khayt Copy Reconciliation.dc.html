How to read the classes
Same — (a) the app and the design mean the same thing. Where the wording differs, keep the app's: it is already translated and already shipped.
New — (b) the design needs a key the app has no equivalent for. Almost all of these are the partial-book vocabulary, which could not exist before the phone held records. Their Arabic is mine and unreviewed — English is the source of truth for these rows; the Arabic is a starting point for a translator.
App only — (c) shipping strings the design does not use yet. Most are the three screens still to do; a few are genuinely obsolete under Bonjour.
