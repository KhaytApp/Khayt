The vocabulary for a partial book

Three patterns, and one rule that decides between them.

1 · Window line
At the end of a windowed list, never the top — the list is true as far as it goes, and the caveat belongs where it stops. Quiet, no rail: the shape of the book is not news.
212 of 3,140 orders
Every unfinished one, and the newest 200 finished. Older history stays on the Mac.
2 · Deferred figure
A number that would be a lie is not rendered as a number. It keeps its slot and its size so nothing reflows — but it is an em-dash in quiet ink, captioned with where the answer lives. Never a zero: a zero is an answer.
18,400
This month
SAR
—
This year
On the Mac
3 · As-of stamp
One strip under the header, not a badge per row. The whole screen is as old as the book is; saying so once is honest and saying it forty times is noise.
The rule that decides

A question bounded by recency is answerable when the window reaches past its boundary.

openPlusNewest(200) guarantees two things: every unfinished order whatever its age, and the newest 200 finished. So "in queue", "printing" and "done today" are always safe — open work all travels, and today's finished work is inside any window.

"This month" is safe only if the oldest finished order the phone holds is older than the first of the month. Here the window reaches back to 14 June, so the month is covered and the year is not. The phone can work that out on its own — it knows the oldest record it holds — which means this is a computed decision, not a guess a screen makes.

Your two conflicts, settled by the code
Clients is a tab. I was wrong.
ContentView.swift ships six, including tab.clients. Five was my number for a companion that was a remote control; a phone that holds clients whole — while orders are windowed — has better client data than order data, and burying its most complete collection one level down would be backwards. Six tabs at 402pt gives 67pt each, which clears the target.
Inventory, not Stock.
The key is tab.inventory, the collection is inventory, the Arabic is المخزون, and the desktop says it too. I picked "Stock" because it is shorter in a tab — a typographic preference, which is not a reason to make the app disagree with its own data model in two languages. Renamed.
What these changes broke
The offline banner was wrong, not just incomplete
It said "Desktop offline — Khayt is not answering" in alarm red. When the phone holds the book, that is no longer a failure: it is the normal condition of a phone in a workshop. Losing the Mac is now a quiet as-of stamp, and red is kept for the shop where the Mac serves no book and the screens really are empty.
"Retry" became the wrong verb
Nothing failed, so there is nothing to retry. The action is to refresh a book that is working fine and merely old.
The 4-step wizard's best screen is gone
Step 4 checked three things and said honestly which failed. Bonjour deletes the two questions it was diagnosing. I moved that screen's job to the pull: it now itemises what actually landed, which both replaces the reassurance and teaches the window before the first list needs it.
Filing an expense writes somewhere you cannot read
expenses is in omitted. The Expense screen you asked for can capture one but must never show a list or a total — it is write-only on this device, and the UI has to say so rather than show an empty ledger.
