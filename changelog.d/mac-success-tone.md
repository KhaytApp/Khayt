- **Mac: Machine profit colours how often each printer's prints came out right.** Under 80% the figure is red, from 95% it is green, and in between it keeps the normal text colour. A printer with too few prints yet is not coloured. The colour follows the per cent shown beside it, so 99% is green and 79% is red, and the figure is always there as well. The rule is shared, so the Windows/Linux app and the iPhone will colour it the same way.
