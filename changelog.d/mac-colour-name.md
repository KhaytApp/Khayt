- **(Maintainers) Colour names, not colour codes: a shared rule.** `lib/colour-name.js` `colourName(hex, lang, { known })` gives the words to show beside a swatch: the spool's own name when it has one (the catalogue's colour name, a tag's), else the nearest of 30 everyday filament colour names by perceptual distance (CIEDE2000, on `lib/color-mix.js`), in all nine languages, else nothing (the swatch alone). It never returns a colour code. 99.9% of the filament catalogue's 16,477 swatches get a name. Bundled for the Mac and the phone (`KhaytEngine.colourName`), with shared vectors. The screens that show colours switch to it next.
