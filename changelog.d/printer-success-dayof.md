- **A printer's success rate counts each print on the shop's own day, in the shared rule (Windows, Linux; Mac and iPhone can now ask for the same).** `lib/printer-success.js` read a print's day as the first ten characters of its time, which is the day in UTC, so for a shop west of UTC an evening print on the last day of a range was left out, and for a shop east of UTC an early-morning print on the first day was too. `successRates` now takes `dayOf(at)` from the caller. The desktop passes `localDayOf`, this computer's calendar, and the Mac can pass its book calendar. Without it the answer is as before. Analytics' "Came out right" column uses it directly instead of its own filter. New vectors cover times on both sides of local midnight, east and west of UTC, 199 of 200 (0.995, never 1) and no runs. **The percentage also never reads 100% unless every print came out right, or 0% unless none did:** 199 of 200 used to show 100%, and now shows 99%. The rule is `successPercent(rate)` in the same file, for every app, and a rate kept to three decimals no longer rounds one failure in thousands up to 1.
