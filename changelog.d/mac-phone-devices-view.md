- **Mac: the owner's phone can read which phones are paired, and whether prices show in the slicer.** Two read-only routes on the native Mac's LAN server, for the iPhone: `GET /api/devices` lists the shop's paired phones (name, role, paired with the PIN or by number, last seen, and which one is asking), and `GET /api/devices/connectors` gives the shop's "Show prices in the slicer" switch and each paired slicer's grant. Owner only: a signed phone whose role is owner, never the PIN alone, an API token or a manager's phone. No key, PIN tag or anything that could enrol or impersonate a device is in either answer, and both are signed. Nothing can be changed through them.
