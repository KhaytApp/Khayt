- **Mac: a print's time-lapse can be kept with the job, when a paired phone asks ("Keep as proof").** The Mac could find a finished print's time-lapse and play it from the printer; it could not keep it, so the proof was gone when the printer cleared its camera folder. It now copies the video into the job's files (`order-files/`, where the Windows/Linux app keeps them, as an `attachedFiles` entry both apps read), by the shared rule that app uses. Two routes on the native Mac's LAN server for the iPhone: `GET /api/orders/{id}/timelapse` says whether a job has one (none, available, kept, or too large to keep), and `POST /api/orders/{id}/timelapse/keep` has the Mac copy it. A video the printer says is over 150 MB, or of no stated size, is not fetched; the limit is on what is read, so a printer that under-states its size is cut off and nothing is left on disk; the file comes only from the job's own printer on the shop's network, with no redirect followed. A signed phone only: reading needs `orders:read`, keeping `orders:write` (two new rows in `lib/device-roles.js`), and at the Mac itself the same act needs orders/edit under the staff lock. Nothing changes on the Mac's own screens yet: a button there needs a frame first.
