- **Mac: the machine sheet shows what each head of a printer holds, and links it to one of your spools.** For a printer that reports it (a Snapmaker U1's spool tags, or its own word for what is loaded), a **Loaded on this printer** section lists each tool: the material and maker from its tag, "Tag read · 1 kg spool" or "Set on the printer · no tag", and the spool it is linked to with what is left. **Link…** / **Change…** opens "Tool N — which spool is this?" with the best match first, the other spools of the same material and colour, and **Add as a new spool** filled in from the tag (you set the price). Khayt never changes a spool by itself: linking only remembers which spool is in a head (the same record the Windows/Linux app and a paired phone write), and a tag that matches nothing changes nothing. The printer is only read. Under the staff lock, linking needs the right to edit a printer and adding a spool the right to add stock. The words are the Windows/Linux app's own, in every language.
