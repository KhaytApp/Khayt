- **Mac: a paired phone can read the spool tags on a printer's heads and say which spool is in one.** Two routes on the native Mac's LAN server, for the iPhone's "A spool tag on a head" (Snapmaker U1): `GET /api/printer/{machineId}/trays` reads the tags (one read-only request to the printer) and matches each against the shelf with the rule the Windows/Linux app's "Which spool is in head N" uses; `POST /api/printer/{machineId}/trays/{head}/spool` records the link on the machine (`headSpools`, the field the other app writes) or adds one spool from the tag, with no price, and links it. An existing spool is never changed, and the printer is told nothing. A signed phone only: reading needs `inventory:read` and writing `inventory:write` in `lib/device-roles.js` (two new rows; an operator up, as adding a spool and editing a printer are at the computer), and a viewer's phone is refused. If the spool was swapped since the phone looked, nothing is written and the phone is shown what is there now. Nothing changes on the Mac's own screens.
