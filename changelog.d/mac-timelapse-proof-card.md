- **Mac: "Keep as proof" on the job page.** A finished job whose printer recorded a time-lapse now shows a card under the job's machine: **Time-lapse found on (printer)**, its size, and **Keep as proof**, which copies the video into the job's own files so the customer's proof stays when the printer deletes its copy. While it copies, the card shows how far it has got and **Cancel**; once kept, **Show** and **Remove…**; a video over the 150 MB a job keeps stays on the printer with **Open on printer**; and if the printer cannot be reached the card says so and offers **Try again**. Nothing is copied unless you ask. Keeping and removing need the right to edit jobs under the staff lock.
