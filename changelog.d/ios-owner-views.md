- **iPhone: the owner can see the shop's paired phones and which slicers may see prices.** Two read-only lists on the owner's phone, on the shop's Wi-Fi. Under Settings › Phones & Wi-Fi: each paired phone's name, how it was paired, when it was last seen and its role, with this phone marked. Under Settings › Connectors: whether prices show in the slicer, and each paired slicer as "May see prices" or "Prices hidden". Nothing can be changed from the phone: roles, removing a phone and slicer prices stay on the Mac. Both lists are shown only from an answer the Mac signed; any other role sees neither.
