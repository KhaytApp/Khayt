- **iPhone: the spool tag in a printer's head, linked to your shelf.** On a machine's Care page, each head whose tag the printer reads (a Snapmaker U1) gets a card: what the tag says, its colour, its weight, and what the Mac matched it to: "Matches your spool …" with Link to this spool and Choose another, or "No spool like it on your shelf" with Add spool from the tag. The colour shows as the real colour in a ringed swatch (one per colour on the tag), with its name beside it when your linked or matched spool names it, and never as a colour code. The Mac reads the tag and does the match; a tap asks it to record the spool in that head, and it refuses if the tag changed meanwhile. Nothing changes a spool without a tap, and an added spool has no price until the shop sets one. Shown only in reach of the Mac; a printer with no tags shows nothing.
- iPhone: a head's colour is named the way the Mac and the desktop name it (shared `lib/colour-name.js`): the spool's own name, else the nearest everyday one ("Black", "Light grey"), never a code. A head the printer reads empty that still has a spool linked shows that spool and says "Not in the printer now" (shared `lib/head-spools.js`).
