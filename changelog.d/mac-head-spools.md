- **(Maintainers) One spool, one head: a shared rule for linking a spool to a printer head.** `lib/head-spools.js` `link(machines, machineId, head, spoolId)` puts a spool in a head and takes it out of every other head, on that printer and every other one; `unlink` empties a head; `shown(linked, reportsEmpty)` says whether a linked head is in the printer or "not in the printer now" (a head the printer reports empty keeps its link until that spool is linked elsewhere). Bundled for the Mac and the phone (`KhaytEngine.headSpoolsLink` / `headSpoolsUnlink` / `headSpoolsShown`), with shared vectors. The Mac's phone route and the desktop's machine card switch to it next; today each writes the head alone, which left one spool in two heads during a watched U1 run.
