- **Mac: Machine profit shows how often each printer's prints came out right.** Beside each machine's hours and margin: its prints in the chosen range ("14 · 3 failed · 1 cancelled", or "not marked yet") and the share that came out right. Cancelled prints are counted and shown but left out of the percentage; a printer with fewer than five decided prints says "Too few prints yet" and shows no percentage, so one failure in two does not read as 50%; a print nobody marked is never a success. Counted by the rule the Windows/Linux app's Machine profit uses, from one result per print run, by the day the run was sent.
