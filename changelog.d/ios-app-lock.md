- **iPhone: a Face ID / passcode lock when Khayt opens, and "Hide job names on the lock screen".** On by default on owner and manager phones (a floor phone keeps no book, so there is nothing on it to lock; the sample shop is not locked). Khayt locks when it starts and again after a minute in the background; Settings › Security offers Immediately, 1, 5 or 15 minutes, and the switch to turn it off. What opens it is the iPhone's own Face ID or passcode, never a Khayt PIN. The lock is its own window, above any sheet, alert or keyboard the app has up, and nothing under it is read by VoiceOver. While the lock is on, the app switcher shows the locked screen, not the book. Widgets, Live Activities and the lock-screen progress are not locked. While the lock is on, Spotlight, Siri and Shortcuts still find jobs and filament, but a job found there names no customer and is not searchable by their name; what Spotlight holds is replaced when the lock turns on or off. "Hide job names on the lock screen" is off by default; when on, print alerts and the Live Activity say "A print" instead of the job's title or the printer's file name, and a request reminder does not name the customer. Decided by Turki on Nov 22; signed frames L1–L4 and L3b. Tests: the default per role, the time away, a failed Face ID then the passcode, no passcode on the iPhone, the alert's words with the switch on and off, the key the extensions read, and the words.
