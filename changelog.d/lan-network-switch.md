- **Windows/Linux: an owner's phone can turn "Make no internet calls" on or off, as from the Mac.** `POST /api/network/switch` on the LAN server, for a signed phone whose role is owner, is the same change the owner makes in Settings ▸ General ▸ Internet use: the same forward-only stamp, the same "tell my other computers" through Khayt Cloud (first when turning it on, after when turning it off), one write, and the open window shows it at once. A stale tap (the switch or its kept list changed since the phone last looked) changes nothing and says what is true now; a purpose this computer does not know is refused, not dropped. Every answer is signed, and `/api/devices/self` tells an owner's phone the route is here (`can: ["network-switch"]`). Documented in `docs/LAN_API.md`.
