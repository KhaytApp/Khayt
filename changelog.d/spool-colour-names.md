- **Windows/Linux: a spool's colour shows as a swatch and its name, never a colour code.** Wherever the app shows a spool's or a tag's colour (the inventory list and its low-stock alerts, the printed spool label, an NFC scan, a printer's heads, the dashboard's low-stock line, a job card's swatch, and the spool lists of the converter, the colour planner and the Color Studio), it says the spool's own colour name when it has one ("Galaxy Black"), else the nearest everyday name in the shop's language ("Black", "Light grey"), else shows the swatch alone. Swatches have an outline, so white and dark colours stay visible, and a screen reader reads the name, never the code. A colour from a model file in the converter, the Color Studio and HueForge keeps its code, where the exact value is the point.
