- **iPhone: a printer's success rate on its page.** A machine's page shows how often its prints came out right over the last 30 days: "23 finished, 2 failed · 3 cancelled not counted" and the rate, or "Not enough prints yet: 3 of 5 needed" with a dash. Counted by the shared rule from the book the phone holds (one result per run; cancelled runs shown but left out; runs with no result never a success). A floor phone keeps no book and shows no card.
